Subtitle Rules Embedded in Video Prompt

IMPORTANT — SPOKEN DIALOGUE AND ON-SCREEN SUBTITLE The character speaks the exact dialogue provided for this scene. DISPLAY THE EXACT DIALOGUE AS AN ON-SCREEN SUBTITLE. Subtitle appearance: - Bold white text - Thick black outline / black shadow - Bottom center - Large and clearly readable - Synchronized exactly with the spoken dialogue Subtitle text rules: - Show ONLY the dialogue - Do NOT show character names - Do NOT show "Emma:" - Do NOT show "David:" - Do NOT show "Sarah:" - Do NOT show "Leo:" - Do NOT add speaker labels - Do NOT translate - Do NOT paraphrase - Do NOT shorten - Do NOT add words - Do NOT remove words - Do NOT create duplicate subtitles - No top captions - No extra text - No watermark - No logo IMPORTANT: The subtitle must appear INSIDE THE GENERATED VIDEO itself, not as separate metadata or a separate script.
